Adaptive Intelligence

Predictions that learn
from what actually happens.

Most forecasting tools show you a line and call it a day. Zen Terminal's Adaptive Learning engine generates 90-day forward close price projections, then recalibrates every remaining prediction the moment you enter a verified actual close.

The system weights your indicator profile to generate forecasts, then applies a sequential correction chain — if reality deviates from the model, the entire forward curve adjusts proportionally.

How It Works

From first ticker
to actionable signal

1

Add your tickers

Build your watchlist in seconds. Zen Terminal pulls price history automatically and begins computing signals against your current indicator profile.

2

Configure your signal weights

Open Indicator Profile, adjust the slider for each technical or fundamental indicator, normalise to 100%, and save. Your weights persist across all platforms via JSON export.

3

Read the composite signal

Each ticker shows a single composite score — positive is bullish, negative bearish — with the individual indicator contributions visible below. No ambiguity.

4

Track predictions against reality

Run the Adaptive Learning engine to generate a 90-day price forecast. Enter actual closes as they arrive and watch the remaining predictions self-correct.

5

Sync privately — your way

Export a complete JSON backup from your desktop, email or AirDrop it to your phone, and import. No accounts. No servers. Information barrier maintained.
